NHS Trust Pressure Board Brief
Public Case Study

A trust-level tool for spotting where severe pressure may build next month.

Using public NHS operational data, this case study shows how trusts can be ranked by next-month risk, how that risk can be explained as demand, flow, or capacity, and how a simple action line can be attached to each flag.

Key Finding

The shortlist captured a large share of the later problem.

Across 13 month-to-next-month tests, the tool flagged the 10% of trusts judged highest risk.

31%
Those flagged trusts later contained about 31% of the very long waits seen in the following month.

What the driver labels mean

Demand means the front door looks busiest.

Flow means patients appear to be moving through the hospital too slowly.

Capacity means bed or space pressure looks most important.

Operational Use

What the tool is designed to support.

It is intended to help focus attention and structure discussions, not replace local judgement.
